Rooted In Kerala Heritage

The Sacred Bharani On The Sunlit Veranda

In our memories of ancestral Kerala homes, pickling was not an industrial rush — it was a sacred calendar of seasons. When early summer brought fragrant country mangoes and crisp wild lemons, stone jars known as Bharanis were brought out, cleansed in boiling water, and dried in the courtyard sun.

Achar & Co. was born to preserve these uncompromised flavours. We do not take shortcuts. We simmer in traditional heavy-bottomed brass vessels, hand-stir in small batches, and let the spices slowly breathe into the fruit.

Why Our Pickles Taste Like Home

✦ ✦ ✦

We remain faithful to the unwritten culinary commandments of Kerala kitchens.

01

Hand-Sorted Produce

Crisp green mangoes, native wild citrons (Vadukapuli), and spicy birds eye chillies (Kanthari) chosen for firmness and natural zest.

02

Pure Gingelly Oil

We exclusively use pure cold-pressed sesame (gingelly) oil, imparting the deep nutty warmth quintessential to authentic Kerala achar.

03

Stone-Ground Spices

Slowly dry-roasted fenugreek, cracked mustard seeds, and vibrant red Kashmiri chillies crushed to release essential aromatic oils.

04

Unhurried Maturation

Never rushed through artificial heating. Matured in glazed ceramic containers to allow the marinade to soften the fruit naturally.
